I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Princeton University. My research is in
labor economics and industrial organization, with a current focus on health
applications.

My work uses large administrative and insurance-claims datasets to understand
how providers, places, and institutions shape access to care and the use of
public programs. Recent and ongoing projects examine variation in children's
mental health prescribing, the take-up of social programs such as SNAP, and the
role of information in emergency care.

You can read more about my research on the [publications](/publications/) page
and download my [CV](/cv/). I'm always happy to hear from people working on
related questions — feel free to reach out by
[email](mailto:gswagel@princeton.edu).
